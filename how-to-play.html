How to Play

Drive around the arena, collect power-ups and take out the bots. Be the last kart standing to win.

Goal

Everyone starts with 3 lives. Each time a kart is knocked out, it loses a life. When a kart has no lives left, it is out of the match. The last kart still driving wins.

Controls

Keyboard only, so play on a computer.

  • ↑ or W Drive forward
  • ↓ or S Brake / reverse
  • ← → or A D Steer
  • Space Use your power-up

Power-ups

Beast

Turn into a black beast for about 5 seconds. You can't be hurt, you drive faster, and you eat any kart you touch.

Rockets

Fires three small rockets in a fan. Each one does part of the damage, so land all three to knock a kart out.

Mine

Drop it behind you. It disappears after 6 seconds if nobody drives over it.

Shield

Blocks one hit. It lasts up to 6 seconds.

Bot levels

Before each match you pick Easy, Medium or Hard. Easy bots are slow and miss a lot. Hard bots drive well and fire often.

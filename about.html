About

Gomtinagar Karts is a free kart battle game that runs right in your browser.

What is this?

It is a college project: a solo kart battle game in the spirit of classic kart-combat games, with its own look, name and style. You race against computer-controlled bots, grab power-ups and try to be the last kart standing.
